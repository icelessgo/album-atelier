# Asset provenance

- `static/brand/album-atelier.svg`: original geometric open-book logo, created for
  this public release; MIT licensed with the code. No business logo was copied.
- `static/mockup.js`: original programmatic cover illustrations and projection
  code. The public version loads only the authorised cover preview and draws its
  own neutral background, book outline and page edge. It uses no supplier image,
  photograph, mask, PSD layer or extracted scene geometry.
- `scripts/create_demo.py`: creates synthetic geometric images locally from
  shapes and text. These are testing illustrations, not wedding photographs.
  Generated output is ignored and never shipped as client data.
- `tests/browser/pixel.svg`: geometric synthetic browser fixture.
- `docs/demo.jpg` and `docs/neutral-covers.jpg`: screenshots of the synthetic
  demo and original neutral cover renderer in this release. They contain only
  generated geometric artwork and sample product values, with no client data,
  supplier imagery or working external proof link.
- `static/brand/*.woff2`: DM Serif Display, Outfit and Caveat fonts, each with its
  corresponding `*-OFL.txt` licence in the same directory.

All Graphistudio-derived mockup images and the original studio logo were excluded
from this export. No supplier PSD, real client photograph, album PDF or screenshot
from a private gallery is included.
